### Added

- A server can cap its turn limit with `OPENBOT_TURN_LIMIT`. The cap only lowers the limit that the memory gives (2, 8 or 16). With the cap set, `OPENBOT_PERSON_RESERVED_SLOTS` slots (3 by default) are kept for a person's messages: routine runs and teammate messages start only while fewer than the limit minus those slots of their own turns run. When the main loop p99 of two minute samples is more than `OPENBOT_SHED_P99_MS` (2000 ms by default), background turns get half of their slots (at least 1), until three samples are under it. Each change logs one line. `OPENBOT_SHED=0` turns this off. `GET /v1/status` and `GET /v1/agents/queue` on the control socket show the effective limit, the memory limit, the cap, the reserved slots, the background limit and the shed state. With no setting, the server works as before.
